A smart fridge at midnight refuses to open — one original roast line, in the fridge's own voice, about its petty curfew. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Midnight Fridge Roast" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## The roast

> It's midnight, so the door stays shut — I didn't spend all day keeping your leftovers cold just to watch you eat them standing up in the dark.

142 characters. The curfew is the setup, "keeping your leftovers cold all day" is the fridge's grievance, and "eat them standing up in the dark" is the punchline — the midnight refusal itself is the joke.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
